  8. 11.16Nov 16周一Mon查看本期View issue

    Peter Yang 连发多条，围绕他与 Anthropic 设计师 Nate Parrott 的访谈展开。Nate 解释了 Claude 为何在新建应用时总倾向于衬线字、米白背景、橙色和卡片边缘的竖条，因为在 Claude 的语境里它只做过这一次，自认为完全原创；Anthropic 因此在 Claude Design 的 prompt 里明确告知模型自身存在这些字体与配色偏好，除非用户要求否则不要使用。Nate 还提到，在 Anthropic 一两天就能验证一个想法，而此前在 The Browser Company 做 Arc 时每个想法要花几周，被压缩的是构建周期，没被压缩的是把产品交到用户手里并验证其是否会被使用的周期。Peter 随后总结了五条让 Claude 远离 AI slop 的要点：提前给上下文、研究熟悉的 UX 模式、让模型意识到自身偏见、先做线框图、要五个方案再混搭。

    Peter Yang shared a detailed interview with Nate Parrott of Anthropic on getting Claude to avoid repetitive design output. Parrott explained that Claude operates in a fresh context and assumes its choices are original: "It's going to be serif text, an off-white background, orange, and these bars on the edges of the cards." The fix is to explicitly tell Claude in the Claude Design prompt that the model over-biases toward certain fonts and colors and should avoid them unless asked. Yang also relayed Parrott's take on prototyping speed: at The Browser Company, each idea took a couple weeks to build out for Arc, but at Anthropic "you can kind of do that in one to two days," though the cycle of putting work in people's hands has not compressed. Yang's top 5 takeaways: give Claude context upfront with screenshots and past work, have it research familiar UX patterns, make it aware of its design biases in a design.md file, start with wireframes, and ask for 5 options then remix them.
