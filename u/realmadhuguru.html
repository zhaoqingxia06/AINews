  5. 10.11Oct 11周日Sun查看本期View issue

    Madhu Guru（Sr Director, AI at Meta）给出一个清晰的框架：Agent 能力将以 RSI 速度演化，而 Agentic security（身份、权限、可观测性）必须跑得更快。他把整个 agents 空间的演化方向概括为 capability -> trust -> autonomy，并指出第一阶段关于能力的问题已推进很远，现在焦点转向信任：系统是否以正确权限采取正确行动，能否被观测、审计并建立问责。

    Madhu Guru frames the whole shift: agent capabilities will soon evolve at RSI pace, and agentic security around identity, permissions, and observability will need to outpace that. The evolutionary direction of the agents space, in his view, runs capability to trust to autonomy. Capability has gotten far, so the focus now moves to whether the harness takes the right action with the right permissions, and whether we can observe, audit, and establish accountability.

  6. 10.10Oct 10周六Sat查看本期View issue

    Madhu Guru（Sr Director, AI at Meta）指出开放权重模型并不是单位智能价格下降的起点，而是既有趋势的顺风。过去几年降价由三个因素驱动：模型厂商把最好的模型蒸馏成更小、推理更便宜的版本；基础设施效率提升；模型提供方之间的竞争。他总结出一个规律：中型模型的第 x 版，智能水平等于大型模型的第 x-1 版，同样的智能、更低的成本。

    Madhu Guru pushed back on the idea that open-weight models caused the drop in price per unit of intelligence, calling them a tailwind on a trend already underway. He credited three drivers: model builders distilling their best models into smaller ones with cheaper inference, infrastructure efficiency, and competition between model providers. His pattern: version x of the mid-size model matches the intelligence of version x-1 of the large model, same intelligence for less money.

  7. 10.9Oct 9周五Fri查看本期View issue

    Madhu Guru 抛出一份创业地图：每一层计算栈都要为 agent 重建。过去几年焦点在让企业软件变得 agent-ready，即 API、connectors、MCP；如今个人 agent 正把同样的讨论推向消费产品，电商只是早期例子。他点名桌面与移动 OS、为 agent 直接运作而建的云基础设施、为成千上万代行 agent 设计的身份权限与安全，以及面向 agent 与人类协作的界面，并建议创业者逐层追问：当主要用户是 agent 时，什么必须改变。

    Madhu Guru lays out a sweeping startup thesis: every layer of the computing stack will need to be rebuilt for agents. Enterprise software has spent years becoming agent-ready through APIs, connectors, and MCPs, but personal agents are now forcing the same reckoning for consumer products, with e-commerce as an early example. He points to desktop and mobile OSes where agents are first-class users, cloud infra built for agents to operate directly, identity and permissions designed for thousands of agents acting on our behalf, and interfaces built for agents and humans together. His prompt to founders: go through each layer and ask what has to change when the primary user is an agent.
