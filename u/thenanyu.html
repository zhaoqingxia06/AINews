  9. 11.9Nov 9周一Mon查看本期View issue

    Nan Yu 给出一个反直觉的工程建议：选“最笨”的方案而不是“最简单”的方案，简单是迭代出来的结果，笨才是正确的起点。他把这条推广到软件的所有层面，架构、设计都该选能用的最笨版本，因为耍聪明意味着用户也得同样聪明才能理解。他还指出软件在你知道要做什么时变简单、不知道时变难，没有清晰度就会花大钱交付一堆垃圾。

    Nan Yu makes the case for starting dumb rather than simple: simple is something you iterate to, while dumb is the correct starting point, and being clever forces your users to be just as clever to understand your cleverness. He extends this to all of software, then notes that software got a lot easier if you know what you wanna build and a lot harder if you don't, since without clarity you ship a pile of junk and spend a lot of money doing it.
