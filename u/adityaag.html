  9. 11.12Nov 12周四Thu查看本期View issue

    Aditya Agarwal 把当下可做的公司分成两类。第一类是 AI 的扩散，相当于今天的垂直 SaaS，无论 FDE 公司、rollup 还是横向产品，本质都是把 AI 送到工作发生的地方，Profound、Cognition、Harvey 都在此列。第二类是 AI 的前沿，用 AI 加速科学进步再从中造产品，他举了 Externa 扩展蛋白质化学构件、Epana Bio 用基因组学与 AI 设计自身免疫疗法、VenomicsAI 从毒液中做药物发现、Equivariant 做半导体多物理建模。他认为第二类被外界严重低估，却将是模型驱动回报的主要来源。

    Aditya Agarwal laid out two kinds of companies to build: diffusion of AI, the vertical SaaS of today (FDE companies, rollups, horizontal products) getting AI close to where work happens, and the frontier of AI, using AI to accelerate science. He cited Externa engineering proteins with new chemical building blocks, Epana Bio designing autoimmune therapies from genomics, VenomicsAI doing drug discovery from venoms, and Equivariant modeling multi-physics for semiconductors, arguing this second category is where most model-driven returns will come from.

  27. 10.8Oct 8周四Thu查看本期View issue

    Aditya Agarwal 长文探讨有抱负的软件工程师今天该做什么。他指出十年前"把软件工程练好"是极其通用的建议，而 AI 让产出可用软件的能力变得普遍，"我能做出来"正成为更弱的优势。他给出三条路径：去做正在吞噬软件的东西；找世界级领域专家组队；或者写 vanilla code 同时牧养一堆 agent，而他认为第三条最没吸引力，因为那只是在同一家公司里做大致相同的工作，只是现在监督编码 agent。他的结论是：仍然要把工程练好，然后刻意选择让这项能力紧挨着什么。

    Aditya Agarwal described his first Waymo ride as a religious experience and talked about it with co-CEO Dmitri Dolgov inside a Waymo. He also laid out three paths for ambitious software engineers now that AI makes producing working software widely available: work on the thing eating software, team up with a world-class domain expert, or write vanilla code by shepherding agents, which he finds least compelling. He advised getting very good at engineering, then being deliberate about what that ability sits next to.
