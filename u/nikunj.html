  22. 10.27Oct 27周二Tue查看本期View issue

    Nikunj Kothari 对 Anthropic 的 S-1 做了细致拆解。他指出截至 6 月 30 日，公司有 2656 亿美元的采购承诺和 3214 亿美元尚未开始的租赁，合计 5870 亿美元。Google 和 Amazon 要求无论用量多少都保底支出，而 SpaceXAI 的协议潜在价值 1205 亿美元，但初始锁定期在 2027 年 1 月和 3 月结束后，双方可提前 90 天取消。他还提到 Broadcom 的 420 亿美元融资安排不直接给 Anthropic 现金，票据以 9% 利息按季计入余额，IPO 后可按 30 日均价的 90% 转股。每美元总算力与基础设施支出对应的收入从 2025 年 Q2 的 0.57 美元升至 2026 年 Q2 的 2.08 美元。

    Nikunj Kothari dug into Anthropic's S-1 compute contracts, finding $265.6B of purchase commitments and $321.4B of not-yet-started leases, a $587B subtotal payable over years. He noted Google and Amazon require minimum spending regardless of usage, while SpaceXAI's agreements carry a potential $120.5B value but can be cancelled on 90 days' notice after initial locked periods end in January and March 2027. Anthropic Direct separates chips, buildings, power and operations, and Broadcom's $42B facility never hands Anthropic cash, instead settling specific equipment rent bills with notes carrying 9% interest. Revenue per dollar of total compute and infrastructure expense rose from $0.57 in Q2 2025 to $2.08 in Q2 2026, and about 80% of H1 revenue was consumption-based.
