  3. 11.23Nov 23周一Mon查看本期View issue

    Swyx 连发多条谈 OpenAI 提议把 perplexity 作为行业自我监管与节奏控制的通用指标，替代 FLOPS。他引述原文称新能力主要来自压缩进展，进入约 0.68-0.7 log-perplexity 区间后应预期更强的能力，并说这张图是讲过去四年故事最漂亮的方式，也说明后训练争论只是大规模预训练这条主线的 B plot。他还质疑“今年 AI 进展超过 2022-2025”这一说法，觉得体感并不明显。此外他推荐了一份从创立到被收购的创始人历程复盘，涵盖 IP 归属麻烦、招聘与裁员建议、如何钓到收购等细节。

    Swyx amplified OpenAI's proposal to make perplexity the universal metric for industry self regulation and pacing instead of FLOPS, quoting the argument that new capabilities emerge through progress on compression and that transformative capabilities should be expected in the ~0.68-0.7 log-perplexity window. He called the accompanying chart one of the most beautiful ways to tell the story of the last four years, and argued that post-training debates are the B plot to the A plot of large-scale pretrain progress. He also flagged a founder journey breakdown covering IP assignment woes, hiring and layoff advice, and how to fish for an acquisition.

  5. 11.18Nov 18周三Wed查看本期View issue

    Swyx 对 Astra 发布的最大批评不在模型本身，而在于没有任何有决策权的人提前否决那支 6.2 广告；他认为模型是扎实的小版本改进，效率是当前三大 RL 目标之一而团队处于世界领先，他每天在用 Astra 跑内容发布管线，并称其支撑了人均超过 1m 收入的精简业务。他更详细地拆解了 Devin bots：跨会话上下文默认呈现，团队为不同项目建立专属 bot，各自拥有独立记忆库与目标，从「Devin 是一群临时 agent」转向「Devin 是一组拥有持久身份与记忆的 bot」。他的核心观点是不要许诺 AI Employees，AGI 尚未到来，真正需要的是单任务之上的下一层抽象，即拥有结果的 agent managers，他已在试用中交出 AEO、QA、跨平台移植、个人行政自动化与视频剪辑。

    Swyx focused his critique of the Astra launch not on the model but on the marketing, arguing nobody with decision-making power vetoed the 6.2 Astra ad. He called the model a solid minor version improvement, praising efficiency as one of the three most important RL objectives and noting he daily-drives Astra as the best workflow automation model he has used, no longer getting stuck in loops and learning through compactions and skill writing; it runs much of the content publishing pipeline for Latent Space and AI Engineer, supporting revenue efficiency above 1m per employee at a bootstrapped business. On Devin, he highlighted that inter-session context is surfaced by default, with teams creating dedicated bots that each have their own memory bank, goals, and long-lived identity, shifting Devin from a swarm of ephemeral agents to a collection of bots. He framed Devin bots as multi-agent teams done right: always on and proactive, owning outcomes not tasks, managing subagents and reviewing themselves, reachable everywhere, and reporting through interactive artifacts he calls devinapps. His aha moment: you do not want "AI Employees" without AGI, you want agent managers that own outcomes, and Devin for Startups offers 65k in credits.
