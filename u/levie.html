    Aaron Levie 长文回应黎曼猜想突破，认为其意义远超数学本身。他引用一段观点指出这并非通常意义上的理论构建，而是某种更怪异、更异质的东西，没有任何人类大脑能独自完成需要 25,000 页漫长路径的虫洞式连接，同时他也把功劳归于 Levent，并认为当前任何 AI 单独也做不到。他总结说，过去解决问题受限于个人或团队能掌握的信息量，而 AI 首次把人类的上下文极限与生产力、发现能力解耦，这可能带来人类产出的质变，从网络安全到生命科学，agent 能把看似无关的工作关联起来，扫描企业内每一份数据来解决问题。

    Aaron Levie wrote that the Riemann Hypothesis breakthrough deserves to be taken seriously well beyond math, quoting a passage describing the solution as "not theory-building in the usual sense" but something "much weirder and more alien," a wormhole requiring a 25,000 page long tour through existing infrastructure with no obvious connection to the goal. He argued AI decouples human context limits from our ability to drive productivity and discoveries, a qualitative shift where every form of prior art, insight, and past research can feed new discovery, useful from cybersecurity to life sciences, with agents correlating unrelated work and scanning enterprise data. The solutions may be less elegant, he wrote, but there will be far more of them.

  15. 11.7Nov 7周六Sat查看本期View issue

    Aaron Levie 认为 AI agent 的推理可以跑在任何地方，因为 AI 对延迟的敏感度相对较低。长时运行的 agent 将成为常态，可以把任务发到远程 AI factory，数据加密进入、被 agent 处理后即丢弃。这使得 AI 数据中心部署地点更灵活，对愿意抓住机会的州或国家是巨大经济机遇。

    Aaron Levie argued that AI agents' inference can run almost anywhere because AI is one of the few areas not as sensitive to latency back to the user. Long-running agents will be the norm, work can be sent to an AI factory in a remote data center with encrypted data combed through and disregarded, and because the economic value of a task is high relative to latency sensitivity, inference does not need to be close to the user. He sees AI factories as ephemeral systems, a huge economic opportunity for states or countries that seize it and a loss for those that forgo it.

  18. 11.1Nov 1周日Sun查看本期View issue

    Aaron Levie 给出一条判断自己是否把 AI 用够的试金石。他说大约六个月前，AI 最多帮你把本来就会产出的东西做得快一点；现在产出应当大幅超出过去的能力，原型不只是能跑能交互，功能本身要更好，工程架构要带来原本追不上的性能收益，研究要穷尽该主题的每一个事实维度，业务流程要能以人类做不到的方式从历次失败中学习。他的结论很直接：如果你做的事在 AI 之前也能做，那你大概没用够。

He said that until about six months ago the best AI could do was reproduce existing output a little faster, but now the output should dramatically exceed what was possible before in a fraction of the time. Prototypes should be fully functional with meaningfully better features, engineering architectures should deliver performance gains otherwise out of reach, research should consume every fact across every dimension, and business processes should operate entirely differently and learn from past failures. He expects AI to be smarter than us on most axes, though intelligence remains jagged.
