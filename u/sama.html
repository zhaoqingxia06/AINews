    Sam Altman 表示各实验室已在 White House Accords 下同意建立提升系统安全的标准与最佳实践，他认为讨论 perplexity 的使用能推进这些目标。他进一步提议前沿模型开发者共同建立评估与红队安全案例的共享规范，并基于 perplexity 等测量设定共同上限，在理解与监控 AI 行为达到具体里程碑（例如 CoT 监控的继任者）之前不得越过。

He proposed that frontier developers jointly set shared norms for evaluating and red-teaming models before first use, plus common limits based on perplexity and other measurements that no one crosses until monitoring reaches a concrete milestone, such as a successor to CoT monitors.
