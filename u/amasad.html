  4. 11.24Nov 24周二Tue查看本期View issue

    Amjad Masad 发布 Replit 关于 agent swarm 缩放定律的新研究：固定两小时窗口下，Opus 5.5 从单 agent 的 3.7 倍加速提升到 32 个 agent 的 18.9 倍；GPT-6.2 Astra 单 agent 最强，但超过 8 个 agent 后出现负缩放。模型会自发形成不同组织结构，去中心化 swarm 比依赖集中协调的扩展得更好，单个模型的智能并不能预测集体智能。Replit 同时开源 Optimization-Bench。他另提到与副总统 JD Vance 会面，讨论美国 AI 领导力、网络安全与 open-weight AI，并称美国可能赢下前沿竞赛却输掉全球 AI 生态。

    Amjad Masad met with Vice President JD Vance on Friday to discuss American AI leadership, cybersecurity, and modernizing software infrastructure, plus American leadership in open-weight AI. His warning: allies want sovereign AI and are increasingly looking to Chinese open models, so America could win the frontier race and still lose the global ecosystem. He also shared new Replit research on scaling laws for agent swarms: given a fixed two-hour window, Opus 5.5 went from a 3.7x speedup on NumPy with one agent to 18.9x with 32 agents, while GPT-6.2 Astra was the strongest solo agent but showed negative scaling beyond 8 agents. Decentralized swarms scaled better than centralized ones, and individual model intelligence proved a poor predictor of collective intelligence; Replit is open-sourcing Optimization-Bench.

  9. 11.18Nov 18周三Wed查看本期View issue

    Amjad Masad 宣布在 Replit 上起一个 agent swarm 只需一句提示。他的论点是智能体集群是 AI 的下一个扩展前沿：多年来靠更大模型与更多思考时间扩展智能，但还有另一个维度，即并行运行大量 agent、探索不同路径、相互挑战并协作，OpenAI 已在数学突破上展示了这种潜力。此前自己实验集群需要搭建复杂编排基础设施，如今任何人都能把集体机器智能变成可编程的东西。

He noted OpenAI has already demonstrated the potential with breakthroughs in mathematics, and that until now experimenting with swarms required complicated orchestration infrastructure. Starting today on Replit, spinning up a swarm of agents is just one prompt away, making collective machine intelligence programmable by anyone.
