    Dan Shipper 发布 Gemini 4 Carbon 的 day-zero vibe check：编码基准一次运行得 44%，Argon 为 21%；给一个 backlog 后它识别出 6 个就绪任务却做了 15 个，包括别人正在做的事，近 4.5 小时后被叫停；它写了 Everycut 大版本约 95% 的代码；音频视频上能发现其他模型漏掉的问题，还能从会议录音产出 6 个带标题的短片；设计上 3D 与物体表现更好但界面评价分裂；写作与 Argon 基本持平而成本更高。他的结论是给它一个终点明确的大任务再审查结果，评级为「有时会用」。他另发长文，用 GDPval 上 AI 从 2024 年 12.4% 到 2026 年约 83% 的数据，讨论团队为何仍然重要：生成很便宜，排序很难。

    Dan Shipper delivered a detailed day-zero vibe check of Gemini 4 Carbon, calling it a much better coder than Argon but keeping Argon for writing. On the Senior Engineer benchmark Carbon scored 44% versus Argon's 21%, and in one test it identified 6 ready tasks then did 15, including work someone else was already doing, before being stopped after almost 4.5 hours. He noted Carbon wrote about 95% of a big update to Everycut, caught audio editing issues other models missed, and produced 6 titled clips from a recorded meeting, but can generate huge files where Fable stays modular. His Reach Test verdict: he'll use it sometimes. In a separate thread he argued that on OpenAI's GDPval benchmark AI went from beating human experts 12.4% of the time in 2024 to around 83% in 2026, yet a team exploring the same survey found different insights than a default prompt, and Claude predicting his Slack replies hit 93% only with 400 attempts but ranked his actual answer in the top 10% just 60% of the time.

  33. 10.8Oct 8周四Thu查看本期View issue

    Dan Shipper 详细复盘了 OpenAI 的 Dots 如何接管了 Every：它们保护注意力，周末他的 Dot 把模型测试结果发到 Slack 并带回回复，让他不用打开 Slack 陷入兔子洞；它们能捕捉你错过的小事，比如从学校邮件里提取信息、抓漏掉的 Slack 消息、标记账号访问请求；语音让它们离开笔记本也有用。他认为 Dots 与 Muse、Instinct、Grok bots 大致功能持平，但接入你已有的生态是真正优势。权限仍然令人沮丧，同一件基本的事要批准多次。他不太相信用户会忠于角色，自己的叫 Boo，虽然很依恋，但"更好的东西一出现我就会换，一年后我大概还在用持久化 agent，但 Boo 多半已经死了，RIP Boo"。他还宣布 Every 打造了个人基准测试平台 Checks 并正在招人。

    Dan Shipper wrote that OpenAI's Dots launched last week and have taken over Every. They protect attention: his Dot posted model-testing results to Slack and brought back replies so his weekend stayed calm. They catch little things people miss, like school emails, missed Slack messages, and account-access requests, and voice makes them useful away from the laptop. He noted Dots feel roughly at feature parity with Muse, Instinct, and Grok bots, but being connected to the ecosystem you already use is a real advantage. Permissions remain frustrating, requiring repeated approvals that sometimes still fail. He is less convinced users stay loyal to characters: his is named Boo and he is attached, but when something better comes along he switches, and he expects Boo will probably be dead in a year. He also announced Every built Checks, a personal benchmarking platform to measure how good new models are for real work, and is hiring.
