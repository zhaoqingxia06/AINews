He frames the OS-internals work as the kind of HCI research he has wanted to do, still full of edge cases.

  27. 10.7Oct 7周三Wed查看本期View issue

    Thariq 判断 Claude 正走向「云端大脑 + 本地双手」的分工：云端负责思考，本地负责操作你的电脑；但比如电脑离线时 Claude 拿不到文件、任务被卡住这类棘手问题还需要解决。他认为在更高抽象层工作始终要求理解下层，coding agent 不会改变这一点，并在 latent space 节目里聊过这个话题。

Working at a higher level of abstraction has always required understanding the lower ones, he noted, and coding agents don't change that. He also discussed this on latent space.
